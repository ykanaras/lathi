Benefits of Greek Olive Oil Soap: Why Choose Lathi?

By Lathi Soap · September 13, 2026

Lathi Soap brings together everyday cleansing, a plant-based soap tradition, and an ingredient with a personal story: extra-virgin olive oil from our family farm in Sparta, Greece.

Choosing a bar of soap can be about more than picking a fragrance. You may want to know where its ingredients come from, who makes it, or whether it fits your preferences. If you are exploring the benefits of Greek olive oil soap, here is what makes Lathi a meaningful addition to your sink or shower.

1. Olive oil with a source you can connect to

Our extra-virgin olive oil comes from our own family farm in Sparta, Greece. That connection is at the heart of Lathi Soap. We use an ingredient tied to our family and our Greek roots, and we share that story with the people who bring our soap into their homes.

For shoppers who value knowing the origin of an ingredient, this gives each bar a sense of place. You can learn more about that connection on our family story page.

2. A vegan choice for your everyday routine

Lathi Soap is vegan, making it an option for shoppers who prefer products made without animal-derived ingredients. Olive oil is a plant-based starting point for our soap. If you have a particular ingredient allergy or preference, contact us about the bar you are considering so you can check the complete ingredients before buying.

3. Handmade character from a family-owned business

We are a family-owned business making handmade olive oil soaps with care and a connection to Greek tradition. Choosing Lathi supports that work and gives you a way to shop directly from the business behind your bar.

It also means there is a person to ask when you want help choosing a scent. Visit our upcoming markets and events to see where you can meet us and explore the collection in person.

4. Scents that make an ordinary moment enjoyable

A favorite fragrance can make washing your hands or taking a shower a small moment to look forward to. Our collection includes options such as herbal-floral Lavender, bright Citrus, and floral Gardenia, along with seasonal selections.

Choose a scent because you enjoy it: perhaps a fresh fragrance for your morning routine or a floral bar for the guest bathroom. Browse the current soap collection for descriptions and availability.

5. A useful gift with a story

Handmade Greek olive oil soap is a practical gift that can still feel personal. Choose a fragrance that reminds you of the recipient, combine a few different scents, or pair a bar with a soap dish for a simple hostess or thank-you gift. The family-farm connection gives you something memorable to share along with it.

What about benefits for your skin?

Soap is a cleansing product, and how a bar feels depends on its complete formula and your own skin. The fact that a soap contains olive oil does not, by itself, establish that it treats a skin condition or is suitable for everyone. We focus on the qualities we can describe honestly: our ingredients, their origin, our handmade approach, and the experience of using our bars.

If your skin is dry or sensitive, fragrance can matter. The American Academy of Dermatology recommends gentle, fragrance-free cleansers for dry skin. Check the ingredients and choose according to your skin's needs.

How to get more enjoyment from each bar

Use a draining soap dish and keep your bar out of standing water between washes. Store unused bars somewhere cool and dry, away from direct sunlight. These simple habits help keep your soap ready for the next use.
